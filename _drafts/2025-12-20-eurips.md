# EurIPS and beyond

EurIPS is over. And what an experience it was. I am still amazed how well everything went, and am grateful that I was able to contribute. 


But first things first: What is EurIPS?

The Informtion Processing Systems - short NeurIPS - is the most prestigious Machine Learning conferences on the globe. It is well established, highly prestigous and very big. (check numbers)
However, especially in this day and age traveling to the USA is not a popular option. Not only is traveling from Europe expensive, resource intensive, it is less manageble for researchers with some commitments to travel. 
some Countries have issued travel warnings.

Participating in large scale conferences on the other side of the globe is a privilege and hence inherently non-inclusive.

So what did I do?
I was co-chair of the affinity workshops, andsuch also got to co-organise the "Women in Machine Learning" workshop.
Excitely, I also got to chair a session of poster prsenters, which meant standing on stage in front of thousands of people. 

Lessons learnt: don't wear big earrings. It turned out that I had to remove my beloved earrings when they put the headset with microphone on me.
